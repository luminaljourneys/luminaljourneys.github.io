I
Wang Si (王思)
Licensed Psychologist · Founder & Lead Practitioner
Founder & Lead Practitioner

Wang Si is a licensed psychologist, quantitative researcher, and corporate executive who healed personal and intergenerational trauma through psychedelics — transforming from an atheist to a spiritual being. As the daughter of Chinese Cultural Revolution survivors, and a queer, polyamorous, neurodiverse woman of color, she brings 30 years of lived experience alongside rigorous professional credentials.

As an immigrant child of parents persecuted in the Chinese Cultural Revolution, her life was shaped by systemic atrocities long before she understood them. She also experienced multiple forms of childhood abuse. It was through psychedelics that she healed personal and intergenerational wounds, transformed from an atheist to a spiritual being, and reclaimed her agency and capacity for expansive love. She didn't just study human transformation — she lived it.

Professionally, she brings the grounded competence of a licensed psychologist, quantitative researcher, and corporate executive. Spiritually and emotionally, she shows up as a modern mystic, using love as the sorcery to magnify the magnificence of clients. Her clinical background gave her the tools to understand the human mind; her research and leadership experiences gave her the rigor to focus on practical outcomes; and her lived experience gave her the empathy, spiritual wisdom, and intuition to meet people in their darkest, most complex places.

Today, she bridges a critical gap in the psychedelic space: the integrity of competence and safety in an unregulated field. She combines licensed professional expertise with 30 years of personal psychedelic experience, so clients don't have to choose between rigorous qualification and real-world experience. She specializes in holding space for seekers, leaders, non-conformists, and deeply feeling humans — whether working to break old patterns, heal complex trauma, deconstruct leadership, grow intimate relationships, or navigate spiritual evolution.

II
Jordan Pierce
LCSW · KAP Certified
Lead Integration Therapist

Jordan specializes in trauma-informed psychedelic integration, working with clients navigating PTSD, chronic grief, and identity transitions. She trained under Dr. Stanislav Grof and leads our weekly group integration circles.

She completed Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy certification through the Integrative Psychiatry Institute and developed the practice's proprietary post-journey mapping framework — a structured tool for translating session insights into sustained behavioral change across the integration period.

Jordan holds an MSW from the University of Michigan and is licensed in California and Oregon. She has presented at the MAPS annual conference and contributed chapters to two edited volumes on psychedelic-assisted therapy. Her personal experience with grief informs her deep attunement to loss as a portal for transformation.

III
Dr. Amirah Roth
PhD · Licensed Clinical Psychologist
Clinical Psychologist

Amirah's research in non-ordinary states of consciousness at Johns Hopkins informs her clinical practice. She has co-authored three peer-reviewed papers on psilocybin-assisted therapy for existential distress.

Published in Psychopharmacology and The Journal of Psychedelic Studies, her work focuses specifically on clients with life-limiting illness and the role expanded states play in shifting relationship to mortality. She integrates CBT and acceptance-commitment therapy with psychedelic facilitation.

Dr. Roth earned her PhD at New York University and completed postdoctoral research at the Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic and Consciousness Research. She holds an adjunct faculty appointment at CIIS and supervises emerging practitioners entering the field.

IV
Marco Silva
RN · Somatic Experiencing Practitioner
Somatic Therapist & Medical Monitor

A former ICU nurse turned somatic therapist, Marco bridges the physiological and psychological aspects of psychedelic work. He monitors medical safety during sessions and leads breathwork and body-centered integration practices.

After nine years in critical care, Marco earned his Somatic Experiencing Practitioner certification through the Foundation for Human Enrichment. His clinical philosophy centers on the body as the primary site of healing — he works with clients to titrate arousal and complete interrupted defensive responses that psychedelics can surface but may not resolve on their own.

Marco holds a BSN from California State University and advanced training in polyvagal-informed care. He is fluent in English and Portuguese and regularly works with clients navigating cross-cultural dimensions of psychedelic experience.

V
Leila Okonkwo
LPC · Certified Facilitator, CIIS
Group Facilitation Lead

Leila weaves indigenous ceremony protocols with evidence-based therapeutic frameworks. Certified through CIIS, she has facilitated over 200 group journeys across the US and Netherlands.

She trained with Mazatec lineage holders in Oaxaca and carries explicit permission to teach those practices in Western clinical contexts. Her LPC training emphasized multicultural competency, which she applies in facilitating a monthly integration circle specifically for people of color navigating psychedelic experiences within contexts of intergenerational trauma.

Leila is fluent in English and Yoruba, conversational in Spanish, and a trained death doula. She incorporates end-of-life perspectives into her work with existential distress — a practice she considers among the most sacred aspects of her clinical role.

Ceremony Indigenous Group Multicultural
VI
Dr. Thomas Wren
MD · Integrative Medicine Fellowship
Integrative Medicine Physician

Dr. Wren's training spans Ayurveda, functional medicine, and psychedelic pharmacology. He conducts all medical screenings and tailors protocols to each client's physiology, medications, and full health history.

He has a particular focus on complex cases involving multiple psychiatric medications and clients previously advised they cannot participate in psychedelic therapy. He has consulted for four psychedelic therapy clinics on developing safe SSRI deprescribing protocols for psilocybin preparation.

Dr. Wren completed his integrative medicine fellowship at the University of Arizona and additional training in Ayurvedic medicine in Kerala, India. He is board-certified in internal medicine and serves on the advisory board of the Psychedelic Medicine Coalition.
