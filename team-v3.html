01 王
Founder & Lead Practitioner
Wang Si (王思)
Licensed Psychologist · Quantitative Researcher · Corporate Executive

A licensed psychologist who healed personal and intergenerational trauma through psychedelics — transforming from an atheist to a spiritual being. She brings 30 years of lived experience alongside rigorous professional credentials to the work of guiding deeply complex human journeys.

As an immigrant child of parents persecuted in the Chinese Cultural Revolution, her life was shaped by systemic atrocities long before she understood them. She also experienced multiple forms of childhood abuse. It was through psychedelics that she healed personal and intergenerational wounds, transformed from an atheist to a spiritual being, and reclaimed her agency and capacity for expansive love. She didn't just study human transformation — she lived it.

Professionally, she brings the grounded competence of a licensed psychologist, quantitative researcher, and corporate executive. Spiritually and emotionally, she shows up as a modern mystic, using love as the sorcery to magnify the magnificence of clients. Her clinical background gave her the tools to understand the human mind; her research and leadership experiences gave her the rigor to focus on practical outcomes; and her lived experience gave her the empathy, spiritual wisdom, and intuition to meet people in their darkest, most complex places.

Today, she bridges a critical gap in the psychedelic space: the integrity of competence and safety in an unregulated field. She combines licensed professional expertise with 30 years of personal psychedelic experience, so clients don't have to choose between rigorous qualification and real-world experience. A queer, polyamorous, neurodiverse woman of color — she specializes in holding space for seekers, leaders, non-conformists, and deeply feeling humans navigating complex trauma, pattern breaking, leadership deconstruction, intimate relationships, and spiritual evolution.

02 J
Lead Integration Therapist
Jordan Pierce
LCSW · KAP Certified

Jordan specializes in trauma-informed psychedelic integration, working with clients navigating PTSD, chronic grief, and identity transitions. She trained under Dr. Stanislav Grof and leads our weekly group integration circles.

She completed Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy certification through the Integrative Psychiatry Institute and developed the practice's proprietary post-journey mapping framework — a structured tool for translating session insights into behavioral change over the integration period.

Jordan's path to this work began with her own loss: the death of a sibling brought her into contact with grief as both a teacher and a portal. She holds an MSW from the University of Michigan and is licensed in California and Oregon. She has presented at the MAPS annual conference and contributed chapters to two edited volumes on psychedelic-assisted therapy.

03 A
Clinical Psychologist
Dr. Amirah Roth
PhD · Licensed Clinical Psychologist

Amirah's research at Johns Hopkins on non-ordinary states of consciousness informs her clinical practice and how she reads the arc of a client's journey — from preparation through long-term integration.

She has co-authored three peer-reviewed papers on psilocybin-assisted therapy for existential distress in life-limiting illness, published in Psychopharmacology and The Journal of Psychedelic Studies. Her clinical work integrates CBT and acceptance-commitment therapy with the expanded states that psychedelics facilitate.

Dr. Roth earned her PhD at New York University and completed postdoctoral research at the Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic and Consciousness Research. She holds an adjunct faculty appointment at CIIS and supervises new practitioners entering the field.

04 M
Somatic Therapist & Medical Monitor
Marco Silva
RN · Somatic Experiencing Practitioner

A former ICU nurse, Marco bridges physiological and psychological dimensions of psychedelic work. He monitors medical safety during sessions and leads breathwork and body-centered integration practices.

After nine years in critical care, Marco earned his Somatic Experiencing Practitioner certification through the Foundation for Human Enrichment. His clinical philosophy centers on the body as the primary site of healing — he works with clients on titrating arousal and completing interrupted defensive responses that psychedelics can surface but may not resolve alone.

Marco holds a BSN from California State University and advanced training in polyvagal-informed care. He is fluent in English and Portuguese, and regularly works with Brazilian and Portuguese-speaking clients navigating cross-cultural aspects of psychedelic experience.

Somatic Breathwork Medical Safety Polyvagal
05 L
Group Facilitation Lead
Leila Okonkwo
LPC · Certified Facilitator, CIIS

Leila weaves indigenous ceremony protocols with evidence-based frameworks, creating containers that are both structured and spacious. Certified through CIIS, she has facilitated over 200 group journeys.

She trained with Mazatec lineage holders in Oaxaca and carries explicit permission to teach those practices in Western clinical contexts. Her LPC training emphasized multicultural competency, which she applies in facilitating a monthly integration circle for people of color navigating psychedelic experiences within contexts of intergenerational trauma.

Leila is fluent in English and Yoruba, and conversational in Spanish. She is also a trained death doula and incorporates end-of-life perspectives into her work with existential distress — a practice she considers among the most sacred work she does.

06 T
Integrative Medicine Physician
Dr. Thomas Wren
MD · Integrative Medicine Fellowship

Dr. Wren's training spans Ayurveda, functional medicine, and psychedelic pharmacology, making him uniquely suited to assess each client's full health picture — including complex medication histories.

He conducts all intake medical screenings, reviews medication interaction risks, and tailors preparatory protocols to each client's physiology. He has a particular focus on complex cases involving multiple psychiatric medications and clients who have previously been advised they cannot participate in psychedelic therapy.

Dr. Wren completed his integrative medicine fellowship at the University of Arizona and additional training in Ayurvedic medicine in Kerala, India. He has consulted for four psychedelic therapy clinics on developing safe SSRI deprescribing protocols for psilocybin preparation. He is board-certified in internal medicine and serves on the advisory board of the Psychedelic Medicine Coalition.
